What I believe about design

  1. Clarity. Every element and every label should say exactly what it does.
  2. Simplicity. Don't overcomplicate it. Find what works and keep the noise low.
  3. Human. People use it, so it should work for them from start to end.
  4. Informed. Data settles it, not opinions.
